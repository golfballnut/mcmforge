## SEARCH METHODOLOGY

For each state, the following searches were conducted:
1. Google: "golf ball recycler [state]", "used golf balls bulk [state]", "recycled golf balls [state]", "lake golf balls [state]", "golf ball recovery [state]"
2. Google Maps: "golf ball recycling" near major cities (LA, San Diego, Palm Springs, Bay Area, Denver, Colorado Springs, Hartford, New Haven, Wilmington, Dover)
3. eBay: "used golf balls" sellers by state/location
4. Facebook Marketplace: "bulk golf balls" in state metro areas
5. Yellow Pages / Yelp: golf ball recycling in state
6. LinkedIn: golf ball recycling companies in state
7. BBB: business profiles for golf ball companies
8. Industry articles and directories

**Total companies identified**: 7 in California, 4 in Colorado (2 local + 2 national w/ local ops), 3 in Connecticut, 0 in Delaware. Plus 6 nearby-state companies that service these areas.
