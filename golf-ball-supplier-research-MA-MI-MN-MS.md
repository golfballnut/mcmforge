### Mississippi Notes
- **No dedicated golf ball recyclers, retrieval companies, or bulk used ball suppliers were found in Mississippi.** This is the weakest market of the four states.
- The Gulf Coast (Biloxi/Gulfport/Bay St. Louis) has award-winning courses that generate ball volume, but retrieval appears to be handled by national companies (PG Golf, Mulligan Golf out of FL).
- Facebook Marketplace and Craigslist (northmiss.craigslist.org) show occasional individual sellers with small lots.
- Nearest regional suppliers: Golf Ball Factory Outlet in Maryville, TN; BallHawker in Green Cove Springs, FL; PG Golf in Sugar Land, TX.
- **Opportunity**: Mississippi is underserved. Direct outreach to golf courses (especially Gulf Coast resort courses) for retrieval contracts or bulk ball purchases could yield supply with little competition.
