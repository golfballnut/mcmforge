## WYOMING

Wyoming has the smallest golf market of any US state -- approximately 50-60 golf courses total, concentrated in Cheyenne, Casper, Jackson Hole, and Laramie. Short season (June-September in most of the state). **ZERO golf ball recyclers, bulk resellers, lake ball divers, or dedicated suppliers found in Wyoming.**

### WY Craigslist/Facebook Activity
- **Craigslist Wyoming:** One listing in Cheyenne for golf balls at $4. Mostly golf clubs for sale.
- **Facebook Marketplace:** Listings found in **Green River, WY** -- appears to be individual sellers, no businesses.
- **Casper:** Durbin Street Golf (local pro shop) and Casper Country Club -- no evidence of used ball sales operations.

### WY Summary
- **Wyoming is a dead zone** for golf ball recycling and reselling. The market is too small and too seasonal.
- **No local competitors, no local suppliers, no divers.** The few courses that exist likely dispose of range balls through national companies or simply don't bother.
- **Not worth pursuing** for procurement unless Links Choice wants to cold-call the ~50 WY courses directly to ask about their lost ball situation (likely low volume and not worth the logistics).
