# GolfBalls.com

## Overview
- **URL:** golfballs.com
- **Business:** Large golf ball retailer selling both new and used/recycled inventory
- **Market position:** Strong brand, broad catalog, significant Google Ads spend
- **Category:** Golf ball marketplace (new + recycled)

## What They Do Well
- **Dual inventory** -- sells both brand-new and recycled/used golf balls in one store
- **Custom logo golf balls** -- personalization service for corporate events, tournaments, gifts
- **Corporate/event sales** -- B2B channel targeting companies, golf outings, fundraisers
- **Strong Google Ads presence** -- heavy paid search investment across golf ball keywords
- **Professional product photography** -- clean, consistent product images
- **Broad brand coverage** -- carries every major golf ball brand (Titleist, Callaway, TaylorMade, Bridgestone, Srixon, etc.)
- **Cross-sell/upsell** -- "you might also like" and bundle suggestions
- **Gift category** -- custom/personalized golf balls positioned as gifts

## What They Don't Do Well
- **Recycled is secondary** -- their used ball selection is not their core focus (new balls drive revenue)
- **No bulk niche** -- they don't specialize in high-volume practice/range bags
- **Premium pricing** -- their new ball inventory means they position higher, not as value-oriented
- **Generic customer experience** -- broad catalog = less specialized feel for recycled ball buyers
- **No plant/sourcing transparency** -- they're a retailer, not a processor

## Where Our Companies Can Compete

### Links Choice Advantages
- **Pure recycled focus** -- authenticity and expertise in the recycled niche vs their broad catalog
- **Plant-direct pricing** -- lower cost structure than a retailer buying wholesale
- **Sustainability messaging** -- "we process the balls ourselves" is more compelling than a reseller
- **Niche authority** -- specialist beats generalist for dedicated recycled ball buyers

### Golf Ball Nut Advantages
- **Brand recognition in recycled space** -- GBN is known specifically for recycled balls
- **300K email list** -- direct access to proven recycled ball buyers
- **Lower price point** -- no new ball inventory overhead means more competitive pricing

### Hot Golf Brands Advantages
- **Bulk specialization** -- 48-ct and 100-ct mesh bags are not a GolfBalls.com focus
- **Value positioning** -- purpose-built for price-conscious buyers and practice facilities
- **Fresh brand** -- can position specifically for the bulk/value segment without legacy baggage

## Key Differentiator: Custom Logo Balls
- GolfBalls.com's custom logo service is a significant revenue stream we don't compete with
- This is a different market segment (corporate buyers, event planners) than our target customers
- Potential future opportunity: custom printing on recycled balls at the plant?
