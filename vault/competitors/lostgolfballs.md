# Lost Golf Balls

## Overview
- **URL:** lostgolfballs.com
- **Business:** Major recycled golf ball retailer
- **Market position:** Well-established brand, strong SEO presence in used golf ball space
- **Category:** Recycled/used golf ball ecommerce

## What They Do Well
- **Large product selection** -- organized by brand (Titleist, Callaway, TaylorMade, etc.) and condition grade
- **Strong SEO rankings** -- top positions for "used golf balls", "recycled golf balls", "refurbished golf balls"
- **Clean Shopify store** -- good UX, clear product photography, easy navigation
- **Email marketing** -- active email campaigns, likely using Klaviyo or similar
- **Trust signals** -- customer reviews, ratings, quality guarantees, money-back promises
- **Condition grading system** -- clear A/B/C grading that customers understand and trust
- **Brand partnerships** -- sometimes works directly with golf ball manufacturers for seconds/overruns
- **Content marketing** -- blog posts targeting golf ball comparison keywords

## What They Don't Do Well
- **Bulk pricing is not their focus** -- they optimize for individual consumer purchases, not 48/100-ct bags
- **Generic branding** -- "Lost Golf Balls" is descriptive but not premium-feeling
- **No plant transparency** -- they don't market their processing/sourcing as a differentiator
- **No sustainability messaging** -- missing the eco/recycling angle that resonates with younger golfers

## Where Our Companies Can Compete

### Links Choice Advantages
- **Vertical integration** -- owns the 20M+ ball/year processing plant (they likely source from third parties)
- **Cost advantage** -- plant ownership means lower cost per ball, more margin flexibility
- **Quality control** -- direct plant ownership = full control over grading and quality
- **Sustainability story** -- "direct from our recycling facility" is more authentic than a reseller

### Golf Ball Nut Advantages
- **300K email list** -- massive re-engagement opportunity (even if unsubscribed from GBN, can reach via HGB)
- **Established brand trust** -- existing customers already know the product quality
- **ShipStation integration** -- efficient fulfillment already in place

### Hot Golf Brands Advantages
- **Bulk niche** -- purpose-built for 48-ct and 100-ct mesh bags (Lost Golf Balls treats bulk as an afterthought)
- **Price point** -- bulk bags can undercut per-ball pricing significantly
- **Target market** -- driving ranges, practice facilities, value golfers (different customer than LGB's individual buyers)
