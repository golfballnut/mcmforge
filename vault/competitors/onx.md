# OnX (DirtSync's Primary Competitor)

## Overview
- **Product:** OnX Offroad -- leading off-road trail navigation app
- **URL:** onxoffroad.com
- **User base:** Massive, well-funded, dominant in the space
- **Rating vs DirtSync:** OnX is a 9/10, DirtSync is a 4/10
- **Category:** Trail navigation, offroad mapping, ride tracking

## What OnX Does Well
- **Offline maps** -- downloadable regions for use without cell signal (the #1 feature in trail navigation)
- **Real-time trail conditions** -- user-submitted reports on trail status, closures, hazards
- **Weather overlay** -- weather data layered directly on the map for ride planning
- **Social features** -- user-submitted trails, reviews, ride sharing, follow friends
- **Professional trail data** -- regularly updated official trail databases
- **Clean, polished mobile UI** -- native iOS and Android apps with smooth performance
- **Community features** -- ride tracking, GPS logging, leaderboards, sharing
- **Ride recording** -- full GPS track logging with stats (distance, speed, elevation)
- **Search and discovery** -- find trails by difficulty, length, type, features
- **Elevation profiles** -- show climb/descent for any trail segment
- **Trip planning** -- save routes, create itineraries, share with group
- **Print maps** -- offline paper backup for deep backcountry

## What OnX Does NOT Have
- **Hatfield-McCoy deep specialization** -- OnX covers everything broadly; DirtSync can go deep on HMC
- **Outlaw trail data** -- OnX only has official/sanctioned trails
- **SxS passability information** -- specific vehicle-type compatibility ratings per trail
- **Hyper-local waypoint categories** -- gas stations, lodging, trailheads specific to trail systems
- **Community knowledge from locals** -- DirtSync can capture "locals only" intel that OnX misses

## Where DirtSync Can Compete
1. **Hatfield-McCoy specialization** -- be the undisputed #1 app for HMC trail system
2. **Outlaw trail data** -- trails OnX won't or can't list (unique differentiator)
3. **SxS passability information** -- "Will my RZR fit?" is a question OnX doesn't answer
4. **Waypoint categories** -- gas, lodging, trailheads with local context
5. **Lower price point / free tier** -- undercut OnX on price for the niche audience
6. **Local community** -- build a tight community around specific trail systems vs OnX's broad approach

## Competitive Strategy for DirtSync

### Phase 1: Own the Niche (now)
- Perfect Hatfield-McCoy coverage (outlaw trails, SxS passability, local waypoints)
- Make DirtSync the obvious choice for HMC riders
- Build community around HMC-specific content

### Phase 2: Close Critical Gaps (next)
- Offline maps (gap #1 -- without this, power users will always prefer OnX)
- Ride tracking (gap #4 -- users want to log their rides)
- Weather overlay (gap #2 -- planning feature)

### Phase 3: Expand (later)
- Add more trail systems beyond HMC
- Social features to build network effects
- Consider OnX-like subscription model

## Pricing Intel
- OnX Offroad: ~$30/year subscription for premium features
- DirtSync opportunity: free tier with premium upsell (undercut OnX for niche users)
