## Cross-Company SEO Strategy

### Keyword Overlap Management
- Links Choice and Golf Ball Nut target similar keywords -- differentiate by:
  - Links Choice = premium positioning ("refurbished", "like new")
  - Golf Ball Nut = value positioning ("cheap", "affordable", "used")
- Hot Golf Brands targets completely different keywords (bulk, mesh bag, practice)
- Avoid cannibalization between Links Choice and Golf Ball Nut

### Shared SEO Assets
- All three golf brands can link to each other (internal link network)
- Plant content ("how golf balls are recycled") can be published on all three
- Customer reviews can be cross-promoted (with permission)

### Link Building Opportunities
- Golf forums (GolfWRX, Reddit r/golf)
- Golf bloggers (product reviews, sponsored content)
- Sustainability/recycling publications
- Local business directories (plant location)
- YouTube golf channels (product reviews, unboxing)
